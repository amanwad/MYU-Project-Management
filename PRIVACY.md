# Privacy Policy

**MYU Project Management LLC** ("MYU", "we", "us") · Effective date: November 13, 2026

This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

## Information we collect
- **Contact information** you give us, such as your name, email address, phone number and mailing address.
- **Project information** you share so we can provide services (schedules, requirements, documents).
- **Communication records**, such as messages you send us and your text message opt-in and opt-out status.

## How we use information
- To provide, schedule and coordinate the services you request.
- To send service-related communications, including email and, if you opted in, text messages.
- To respond to inquiries, provide support and improve our services.
- To meet legal, tax and accounting obligations.

## SMS / text messaging
- We send text messages only to people who have opted in.
- **Mobile information will not be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.** Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties.
- We may use service providers (for example, an SMS delivery platform such as Twilio) solely to deliver messages on our behalf.
- Reply **STOP** to opt out at any time, or **HELP** for help. Message and data rates may apply. Message frequency varies.

## Sharing
We do **not** sell or rent your personal information. We share information only with:
- service providers that help us operate (e.g., messaging, email, hosting), bound to use it only for our purposes;
- partners or vendors working on your project, only as needed and with your knowledge;
- authorities when required by law.

## Retention & security
We keep information only as long as needed for the purposes above or as required by law, and we use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards to protect it.

## Your choices
You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information, and you may opt out of text messages (reply STOP) or emails (use the unsubscribe link or contact us) at any time.

## Children
Our services are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

## Changes
We may update this policy; the effective date above will change when we do.
